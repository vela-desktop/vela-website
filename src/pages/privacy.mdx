The short version: velade.org doesn't use cookies, doesn't show ads, and doesn't send anything about you to Google, Meta or any other third party. To see which pages are read, the site uses [Umami](https://umami.is/), a privacy-friendly analytics tool that is free software, like Vela. It runs on our own server.

## Analytics

Umami counts visits without cookies and without identifying you. For each page view it records:

- the page, and the site you came from, if any;
- your browser, operating system and type of device, and your screen size;
- your language, and your country, worked out from your IP address.

Your IP address itself is not stored. To count unique visitors, Umami combines it with your browser and a secret value that changes every month, then keeps only a hash of the result. It can't be traced back to you, it can't follow you to other sites, and a new month starts from scratch.

Umami can also record some visits as a replay: how the page was laid out, where you scrolled, moved the mouse and clicked. It helps us see where the site is confusing. The site has no forms and no accounts, so there is nothing personal to type, and replays are never shared with anyone.

Umami is [open source](https://github.com/umami-software/umami) under the MIT license: anyone can read exactly what it collects. Our instance runs on a server we manage ourselves, and the data stays there.

## Server logs

Like every web server, ours writes a log line for each request: IP address, time, the page requested, your browser, and the referring page. The logs are used only to keep the site running and secure, and are deleted automatically after about two weeks.

## Everything else

Fonts, images and scripts are all served from velade.org itself. Nothing is loaded from other sites, except the Umami script above.

Links to GitHub take you to GitHub, where [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies.

## Opting out

The site works exactly the same without analytics. If you block `analytics.brandodev.it` with a content blocker, or browse with JavaScript turned off, no visit is recorded.
