In [the first article](/blog/why-vela/), I said I don't like to customize my desktop.

This is not in fact 100% correct. I have strong opinions about how I want my desktop, but I also understand that I have strong opinions.

Let me explain: my aim is not only to satisfy myself; it's to build something for most people. This means that even if this desktop environment will of course follow my taste, and customization will not be a priority, especially in the first weeks and months of development, **customization will be part of this desktop.**

## Already there

Some of it you can already change today:

- **Light and dark modes**, which KDE and GTK apps follow too.
- **Wallpaper and colors**, in *Settings → Personalization*.
- **The taskbar**, centered or aligned to the left.

More will come.

## What's coming

Because Vela is way more than a customized shell. And I'd never want anyone to give up on Vela just because they don't like my theme. So there definitely will be something like KDE themes.

<Aside title="What about tiling?">
  I've also thought about a tiling window manager version. It's not something I can take on myself, but Vela is free software: if you want to build it, [open an issue](https://github.com/vela-desktop/vela/issues) and let's talk.
</Aside>
